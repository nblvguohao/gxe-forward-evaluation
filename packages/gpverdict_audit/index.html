GPverdict Audit

Audit how a genomic prediction model was tuned — from its candidates' predictions, in your browser.

Was it chosen on the reported data?

Checks whether the hyperparameters, epoch or model were chosen on the environments or years that are reported.

Is the criterion admissible?

Where the pick of a pooled criterion (pooled Pearson or loss) ranks on within-environment Spearman.

Where does the pooled score come from?

The share an environment-mean fit explains (φ) and the within-environment scale relative to its optimum u*.

Does the lead survive?

Optimism from choosing on the reported data, and the difference from a strong baseline with its resolution.

One row per candidate, environment and genotype with columns candidate (configuration, epoch or model), environment, genotype, observed, predicted. Optional set column: selection for the predictions the choice was made on, report for the reported ones (default).
